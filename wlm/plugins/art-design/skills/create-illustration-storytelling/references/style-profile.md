# Style Profile: Illustration and Storytelling

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `14_Illustration-and-Storytelling`
- Coverage: 2,987 projects and 3,141 media files
- Common production evidence: Photoshop-led digital 2D painting with occasional PaintTool SAI, Clip Studio Paint, Procreate, and traditional drawing
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- single narrative beat
- editorial or cover illustration
- character relationship scene
- stylized splash illustration

## Visual grammar

- Choose one story beat and one emotional focal relationship.
- Use gesture, eyelines, overlap, and light to direct reading order.
- Let background objects imply before and after without explaining everything.
- Mix polished focal rendering with graphic or painterly simplification elsewhere.

## Technique workflow

- Write the moment as a sentence containing subject, action, and change.
- Thumbnail three staging options with different camera distance.
- Resolve value hierarchy and gesture before surface polish.
- Use color script and edge control to protect the narrative focal beat.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Narrative illustration capturing the moment when [subject/action/change], clear emotional relationship between [focal elements], gesture and eyelines directing a readable sequence, [camera distance and composition], background clues implying context, controlled color script and lighting, polished focal rendering with simplified supporting shapes.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid poster posing when a story is requested, equal emphasis on every character, explanatory text, decorative backgrounds with no narrative role, ambiguous eyelines, and effects that obscure the emotional beat.
```

## Quality checks

- The story beat can be stated in one sentence.
- Reading order is intentional.
- Gesture and expression agree.
- Supporting detail implies context without stealing focus.
