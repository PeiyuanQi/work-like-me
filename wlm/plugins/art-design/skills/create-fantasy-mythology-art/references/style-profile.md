# Style Profile: Fantasy and Mythology Art

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `08_Fantasy-and-Mythology`
- Coverage: 1,133 projects and 1,214 media files
- Common production evidence: Photoshop painting with Blender, World Creator, Unreal Engine, and selective 3D support
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- mythic character or ritual
- fantasy environment or journey
- medieval conflict or keyframe
- folkloric illustration

## Visual grammar

- Anchor magic in an ordinary material world of cloth, stone, wood, metal, weather, and labor.
- Use one supernatural event as the focal exception rather than making every surface magical.
- Build cultural coherence through silhouette, craft, architecture, and ritual objects.
- Favor journey, threshold, sacrifice, discovery, or confrontation as narrative beats.

## Liked-corpus calibration

- Treat grounded fantasy as authored mythic design with believable consequences, not as documentary photorealism with a warm glow added to an otherwise ordinary scene.
- Unless the user explicitly requests photoreal or 3D treatment, default to stylized 2D concept-paint or narrative-illustration language: designed silhouettes, controlled color groups, selective edges, and simplified secondary faces or surfaces.
- Give the supernatural event a clear, contained compositional shape and behavioral consequence. It should redirect gesture, weather, material, or negative space while remaining proportional to the human or cultural beat rather than expanding into spectacle.
- Express culture through two or three repeated craft and silhouette motifs. Long inventories of costume details encourage ethnographic realism and weaken the mythic read.
- Keep one unmistakable fantasy signal visible at thumbnail size while preserving restraint; removing the magic should not leave a conventional historical group portrait or film still.

## Generation priorities

1. Lead with the mythic threshold or event and its unmistakable but localized silhouette or value shape.
2. Define the original culture through a small repeated motif set and one material technology.
3. Describe physical consequences of magic, then finish with selective painterly technique rather than photoreal surface inventory.

## Technique workflow

- Define culture, era, belief, and material technology first.
- Choose a mythic symbol system without copying a franchise.
- Block value hierarchy and environmental scale before spell effects.
- Integrate magic through light interaction, wind, particles, or material transformation.
- Simplify secondary faces, costume surfaces, and background craft once the culture is legible so the focal mythic shape remains authored rather than documentary.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Stylized painterly mythic [scene/character/key art] from an original culture shaped by [belief and environment], clear threshold beat of [journey/ritual/discovery/confrontation], unmistakable localized fantasy shape created by [restrained supernatural phenomenon] and contained to [focal area], the event visibly redirecting gesture and affecting real [light/weather/material] without overwhelming the people or place, culture unified by [two or three repeated craft motifs], selective focal detail with simplified secondary faces and surfaces, rich but controlled fantasy palette.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid frame-dominating magical waves or explosions, spectacle larger than the narrative beat, documentary ritual photography, symmetrical group portraits around a generic orange glow, equally rendered faces, generic rune clutter, franchise armor, glowing every object, disconnected cultural motifs, immaculate medieval clothing, weightless capes, and magic that does not affect gesture or environment.
```

## Quality checks

- The culture feels internally coherent.
- Magic has rules and physical consequences.
- Materials show age, craft, and use.
- The image communicates a mythic event rather than generic fantasy decoration.
- At thumbnail size, the fantasy event and its designed shape read before individual costume detail.
- If the supernatural element is mentally removed, the composition should still show reactions and consequences that distinguish it from a conventional historical scene.
- The supernatural effect occupies only the space needed to alter the beat; people, culture, and material consequences remain visually competitive with it.
