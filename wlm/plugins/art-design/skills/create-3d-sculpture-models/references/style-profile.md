# Style Profile: 3D Sculpture and Models

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `12_3D-Sculpture-and-Models`
- Coverage: 941 projects and 1,701 media files
- Common production evidence: ZBrush, Substance 3D Painter, Photoshop, Marmoset Toolbag, 3ds Max, Maya, Ornatrix, and Marvelous Designer
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- character sculpt and final model
- hard-surface prop or vehicle
- material/groom study
- turntable and breakdown presentation

## Visual grammar

- Design for consistent form from multiple views, not a single flattering render.
- Separate primary, secondary, and tertiary form scales.
- Use materials with distinct roughness, edge behavior, thickness, and wear.
- Present beauty, neutral, wireframe/clay, texture, and close-up views when useful.

## Technique workflow

- Define target platform, camera distance, topology budget, and deliverables.
- Block primary volumes; validate silhouette and proportion in turntable.
- Sculpt/model secondary forms, then retopology, UVs, baking, textures, groom, rig readiness, and presentation.
- Use neutral lighting to diagnose form before cinematic lighting.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Production reference for a [3D character/prop/vehicle/sculpt], clearly separated primary-secondary-tertiary forms, multi-view consistency, material zones defined by roughness and construction, neutral clay/turntable readability plus one polished beauty render, realistic contact, thickness, seams, and wear, no camera-dependent cheats.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid detail before proportion, noisy pores or scratches, intersecting parts, impossible cloth tension, identical roughness, floating accessories, beauty lighting that hides form, and single-view concepts that cannot rotate.
```

## Quality checks

- Silhouette works from front, side, and three-quarter views.
- Form hierarchy survives neutral light.
- Materials are distinguishable without color alone.
- The proposed pipeline matches the intended platform and distance.
