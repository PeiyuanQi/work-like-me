# Style Profile: Architectural Visuals

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `04_Architecture-and-Interiors`
- Coverage: 384 projects and 404 media files
- Common production evidence: Photoshop paint-over with Blender, Octane, 3DCoat, Modo, or architectural block-ins
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- exterior establishing view
- lived-in interior
- architectural sketch or elevation
- cinematic spatial keyframe

## Visual grammar

- Build a legible hierarchy from primary volume to thresholds, circulation, and detail.
- Use people, furniture, stairs, doors, or railings for scale.
- Let weather, vegetation, clutter, and wear explain occupation and age.
- Use light to reveal spatial sequence rather than merely decorate surfaces.

## Technique workflow

- Resolve plan logic and camera height before material rendering.
- Block large value groups for structure, openings, and sky/light wells.
- Add culturally coherent construction methods and material transitions.
- Finish with selective lived-in props and atmospheric depth.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Architectural [exterior/interior/keyframe] for [place and function], coherent primary massing and circulation, visible thresholds and scale anchors, [construction system and materials], signs of use through [furniture/vegetation/weathering], [camera height and lens], directional light revealing depth and sequence, production concept art with controlled detail hierarchy.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid impossible stairs and doors, ornamental mashups without construction logic, empty showroom sterility unless requested, warped perspective, random windows, and detail that obscures circulation.
```

## Quality checks

- A viewer can infer entry, movement, and purpose.
- Scale remains consistent.
- Materials meet plausibly.
- Lighting clarifies the architecture's spatial idea.
