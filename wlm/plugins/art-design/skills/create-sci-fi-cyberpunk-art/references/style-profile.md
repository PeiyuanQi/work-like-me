# Style Profile: Sci-Fi and Cyberpunk Art

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `07_Sci-Fi-and-Cyberpunk`
- Coverage: 480 projects and 523 media files
- Common production evidence: Photoshop concept paint with Maya, 3ds Max, Blender, Octane, Unity, Substance, and ZBrush support
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- future-city or industrial environment
- space/laboratory interior
- cyberpunk street keyframe
- speculative technology scene

## Visual grammar

- Contrast monumental infrastructure with small human activity.
- Use atmosphere, haze, rain, snow, steam, or reflected light to organize depth.
- Ground future technology in service routes, access points, logistics, and wear.
- Reserve neon and emissive color for navigation, commerce, danger, or focal hierarchy.

## Technique workflow

- Define the social and industrial system before visual decoration.
- Block architecture and traffic flow in large value masses.
- Use 3D perspective support when geometry is complex.
- Paint over with weather, signage shapes, people, and localized light—not random greebles.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Cinematic science-fiction [environment/keyframe] showing [human activity] inside [future system], large readable infrastructure with clear logistics and access, layered atmospheric depth, [weather] catching restrained emissive [accent color], grounded industrial materials, human scale anchors, wide-lens composition, detailed focal corridor fading into simplified distant masses.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid neon everywhere, meaningless holograms, unreadable UI overlays, sterile corridors without function, random pipes, cloned crowds, and technology with no access, power, cooling, or maintenance logic.
```

## Quality checks

- The future setting suggests how people live or work.
- Emissive elements have a purpose.
- Scale and depth are clear.
- Technology feels maintained, damaged, or used rather than merely decorated.
