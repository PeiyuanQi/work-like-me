# Style Profile: Sketch and Study Art

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `02_Sketches-Studies-and-Anatomy`
- Coverage: 1,499 projects and 1,512 media files
- Common production evidence: Mostly Photoshop and Procreate-style 2D workflows, plus pencil/ink language
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- gesture and anatomy study
- portrait or lighting study
- environment/value thumbnail
- material, color, or motion study

## Visual grammar

- Preserve construction lines, decisive marks, and visible problem solving.
- Concentrate fidelity on the study target and simplify everything else.
- Use neutral paper, white canvas, or a single toned ground unless color is the subject.
- Prefer rhythm, weight, and value grouping over decorative finish.

## Liked-corpus calibration

- The liked subset favors authored light, color, portrait, and environment practice as well as drawing studies; preserve painterly grouping and visible brush decisions instead of defaulting to a clean anatomy diagram.
- In multi-attempt gesture sheets, keep at least half the attempts at line-of-action, simple-volume, or single-shadow-mass stages. Vary scale, cropping, corrections, and finish level.
- Make phase or question diversity visible in silhouette and value, not through infographic arrows or evenly polished repetitions.
- Treat a tidy character-design sheet as a failure mode unless the user explicitly asks for production model-sheet polish.
- Do not let one large, fully rendered figure turn a comparison sheet into character key art. The most finished attempt should still expose construction, corrections, or unresolved edges and should not dominate the page.
- Preserve irregular paper rhythm and negative space. Mix line, broad value, and limited accent handling so the page reads as accumulated inquiry rather than repeated final poses.

## Generation priorities

1. Put the learning question and the deliberately unfinished medium in the first sentence.
2. Define the contrast between attempt stages before describing anatomy, costume, or facial detail.
3. Keep subject description lean; excess character detail encourages the generator to polish a hero figure instead of showing process.

## Technique workflow

- State one study question before prompting.
- Limit the brush family and palette to prevent accidental over-rendering.
- Use large-to-small passes: gesture, structure, shadow mass, selective accents.
- Keep multiple attempts on one sheet when comparison is useful.
- For a study sheet, reserve at least two-thirds of the attempts for exploratory stages and keep every face, hand, costume, or surface subordinate to the learning question.

## Prompt patterns

Single-study mode:

```text
Focused [portrait/lighting/value/color/material/environment] study of [subject], exploring [single learning goal], visible construction or brush decisions, [paper/toned ground], large value or color masses, selective edges and corrections, fidelity concentrated only on the study target, intentionally unresolved secondary areas rather than promotional polish.
```

Comparison-sheet mode:

```text
Focused [gesture/anatomy/value/color/material] comparison study of [subject], exploring [single learning goal], [paper/toned ground] with generous negative space, irregularly grouped attempts spanning line-of-action, simple-volume, and broad shadow or color block-ins, visible corrections and economical marks, no dominant finished hero figure, at least two-thirds of the attempts remaining exploratory, intentionally incomplete studio practice rather than character key art.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid a large fully rendered central character, uniform polish, repeated equally finished poses, rigid model-sheet grids, airbrushed surfaces, excessive micro-detail, cinematic effects that hide structure, decorative frames, fake signatures, and making every edge equally sharp.
```

## Quality checks

- The learning objective is visible without explanation.
- Marks show structure and decision making.
- The study has a clear focal area and intentional unfinished zones.
- Values remain readable in grayscale when value is relevant.
- In comparison-sheet mode, cropping out the surrounding attempts must not leave a convincing standalone character illustration; if it does, reduce finish and restore comparative process evidence.
