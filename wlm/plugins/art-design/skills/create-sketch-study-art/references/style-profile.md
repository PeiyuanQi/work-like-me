# Style Profile: Sketch and Study Art

## Corpus basis

- Source category: `02_Sketches-Studies-and-Anatomy`
- Coverage: 1,499 projects and 1,512 media files
- Common production evidence: Mostly Photoshop and Procreate-style 2D workflows, plus pencil/ink language
- Provenance boundary: derived from aggregate metadata and representative visual inspection on 2026-08-02; no source artwork is bundled with this skill.

## Best-fit modes

- gesture and anatomy study
- portrait or lighting study
- environment/value thumbnail
- material, color, or motion study

## Visual grammar

- Preserve construction lines, decisive marks, and visible problem solving.
- Concentrate fidelity on the study target and simplify everything else.
- Use neutral paper, white canvas, or a single toned ground unless color is the subject.
- Prefer rhythm, weight, and value grouping over decorative finish.

## Technique workflow

- State one study question before prompting.
- Limit the brush family and palette to prevent accidental over-rendering.
- Use large-to-small passes: gesture, structure, shadow mass, selective accents.
- Keep multiple attempts on one sheet when comparison is useful.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Focused [gesture/anatomy/value/color/material] study of [subject], exploring [single learning goal], visible construction and confident economical marks, [paper/toned ground], large shadow masses, selective edges and accents, intentionally incomplete outside the focal problem, studio practice sheet rather than finished promotional art.
```

## Avoidance pattern

```text
Avoid uniform polish, airbrushed surfaces, excessive micro-detail, cinematic effects that hide structure, decorative frames, fake signatures, and making every edge equally sharp.
```

## Quality checks

- The learning objective is visible without explanation.
- Marks show structure and decision making.
- The study has a clear focal area and intentional unfinished zones.
- Values remain readable in grayscale when value is relevant.
